# Contributing

This tap is a generated, one-way mirror. `Formula/bagdock.rb` is rendered and
pushed by an automated sync job in [bagdock/bagdock-cli](https://github.com/bagdock/bagdock-cli)
whenever a new CLI version is released; nobody edits it here directly.

Because of this, pull requests opened here cannot be merged. Any PR will be
closed automatically with a comment pointing back to bagdock-cli.

## Reporting bugs and requests

Issues are welcome and read. If `brew install`/`brew upgrade` fails, or the
formula is out of date, open an Issue here — it will be triaged and, where
relevant, carried into the bagdock-cli tracker.

## Releases

The formula's version, download URL, and sha256 are all derived from the
corresponding `@bagdock/cli` npm release; nothing here is hand-maintained.
